# DD House — Pickup

- **Pickup Location**: Pickup occurs at the DD House store (Venkat Nagar, Kakinada). Customers must collect their order from the store.
- **Order ID and PIN Verification**: An Order ID and a PIN are generated upon order placement. The customer must provide these relevant order identification details at the store counter to collect their order.
- **Pickup Delay Handling**: If the customer informs the store early about a pickup delay, the store attempts to keep the item safe. If necessary and appropriate, a fresh item may be prepared when the customer arrives.
- **Maximum Holding Duration**: The exact maximum holding duration is TBD.
