# DD House — Ordering Policies

- **Website Ordering**: Online ordering is supported through the DD House website.
- **WhatsApp Ordering**: Ordering through WhatsApp is NOT supported. The WhatsApp number is for contact only.
- **Instagram Ordering**: Ordering through Instagram is NOT supported.
- **Multiple Products Ordering**: Customers can order multiple products in a single order.
- **Pickup Time Selection**: Customers can select their preferred pickup time when placing an order.
- **Same-Day Orders**: Same-day orders are supported at DD House.
- **Pre-Booking Orders**: Pre-booking orders is supported. Customers can pre-book at least 4 hours in advance.
- **Preparation Time**: Normal preparation takes 15 minutes. During heavy rush, preparation takes up to 20 minutes.
- **Pickup and Delivery Policy**: DD House currently supports store pickup rather than delivery. Customer MUST pick up the order from the store.
