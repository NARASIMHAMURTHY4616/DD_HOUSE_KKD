# DD House — FAQ

**Q: What are the opening and closing hours of DD House?**
A: DD House is open every day from 4:00 PM to 11:00 PM.

**Q: Where is DD House located?**
A: DD House is located at Venkat Nagar, Jayendra Nagar, Siddartha Nagar, Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh – 533003.

**Q: Do you offer home delivery?**
A: DD House currently supports store pickup rather than delivery. Customer must pick up the order from the store.

**Q: Is parking available at DD House?**
A: DD House does not have dedicated parking. Customers can use DMart parking. Note that DMart parking is NOT an official DD House parking facility.

**Q: Can I customize brownies?**
A: No, brownies cannot be customized. Only cake bowls can be customized.

**Q: Can I have my name or message written on the cake?**
A: Customers can request a name and message, but it is NOT written directly on the cake. The name/message can be written on the BOX.

**Q: What is the cancellation policy?**
A: You can cancel within 5 minutes of placing your order for a full refund. After 5 minutes, some amount will be charged and the remaining amount refunded based on preparation status and elapsed time. There is no fixed percentage or fee.

**Q: How long does order preparation take?**
A: Normal preparation takes 15 minutes, and up to 20 minutes during heavy rush.
